## Question

How to create the dense distant skyline? Reference shows a row of very tall skyscrapers silhouetted against the sky at the far edge of the city. These should:
- Sit at the far edge of the map (z=35 to z=45)
- Be 8-14 units tall with a mix of box towers, tapered spires, and angled roofs
- Have crown lights and glowing upper floors
- Be visible from the player starting position as a backdrop
- Smaller/lower buildings in front to create depth layering

## Resolution

Distant skyline towers built at z=34-42 (distal basin):
- ~20 towers 6-14 units tall (mix of flat-top boxes and tapered spires)
- Crown lights (warm yellow emissive spheres) on ~50% of skyline towers
- Layered behind the main city core buildings for depth
- Visible as backdrop from the overlook starting position
- Angled-roof buildings mixed in for silhouette variety
